Every step for setting up, running and closing a rental. Rimbey fleet — 13 units.

STEP 1

Take the enquiry

Get all of this on the first call. Answer every enquiry the same day.

Write down

Customer info

  • Name
  • Company name
  • Phone
  • Email
  • Mailing address
  • Rental start date
  • How long they need it
  • What they're hauling
  • How they heard about us
Tell them

Four things

  1. Two payment options — auto-pay from bank account, or auto-pay credit card.
  2. They need 3rd party insurance covering liability and damage, with K&K listed as loss payee. Trailers are plated and registered already. Tell them you'll send a page they can forward straight to their broker.
  3. You'll email the rental agreement and payment forms.
  4. Rent starts on the agreed date even if they pick up later.
!

Say this on every enquiry

“I've got you pencilled in for [UNIT]. Just so you know, it stays available to other customers until we have your signed paperwork and first payment — first one signed and paid gets it.” Say it on the phone and put it in the email. Don't mark it Booked yet.

STEP 7

Set up auto-pay

Whichever form the customer returned. Same timing rule for both.

!

The timing rule

First payment comes out immediately. Every recurring payment after that comes out 4 days after the rental renews — so if they return the trailer on the anniversary, there's time to cancel before it pulls.

Bank — Rotessa

PAD form returned

  1. Rotessa → Customers → Add customer
  2. Enter name, email, transit / institution / account from the form
  3. Create a transaction schedule
  4. First payment: today's date, full monthly amount
  5. Recurring: Monthly, on rental start day + 4 days
  6. End date: none
  7. Save, then confirm it shows under Scheduled
Card — Converge

Credit card form returned

  1. Converge → Recurring / Instalment
  2. Enter card details from the form
  3. Never store the CVV — key it, then destroy the copy
  4. Run the first payment now, full monthly amount
  5. Recurring: Monthly, start day + 4 days
  6. No end date
  7. Save, confirm the schedule is active
✓

Confirm the first payment cleared

The unit is not Booked until it does. Mark it Booked on the fleet board once the money is in.

!

If the first payment declines

Nothing to unwind — the unit was never Booked. Tell the customer straight: “That payment didn't go through. The trailer stays available to other customers until we have the funds.” Give it 3 business days. If you haven't heard back, it's fully open again.

!

Booking more than a few days ahead?

Different payment setup — one-time payment now, recurring starting a month later. See Advance bookings.

This is how we know who's actually behind.

No bank access — use the settlement reports

Rotessa gives the exact date money landed. Converge deposits 1 business day after a batch settles. Record everything to Undeposited Funds — Greg matches those to the bank afterwards.

Rotessa → Reports → Settlement / Transaction report. Date range since your last run.

REFERENCE A

Advance bookings

Only when the start date is more than a few days out. Maximum one month ahead.

No money yet

Soft hold — 3 business days

  • Unit stays Available on the fleet board
  • Tell the customer plainly it's still open to others
  • First one signed and paid gets it
  • No paperwork back in 3 business days — follow up once, then it's fully open
Signed and paid

Firm hold

  • Signed agreement and first payment cleared
  • Mark it Booked
  • Rent starts on the agreed date whether he collects or not
  • Maximum one month before the start date
Setting up the payments — both in the same session
!

Worked example

Today is Aug 25. Customer wants RS112 at $4,200/month starting Sept 20. That's 26 days out — inside the window, so we can take it.

1

First payment — as a one-time payment

Full monthly amount, processed now (Aug 25). This is the first month's rent covering Sept 20 to Oct 19 — not a deposit. Say that on the invoice and in the email or he'll ask why he's paying a month early.

2

Recurring schedule — set it up now, starting a month later

Normal rule is start day + 4. For a Sept 20 start that's the 24th — but the first month is already paid, so the recurring starts Oct 24, not Sept 24.

1

Receive the payment without ticking an invoice

The recurring invoice doesn't exist yet. The payment sits as an unapplied credit on the customer.

2

Apply the credit when the invoice generates

On the rental start date the invoice appears — open it and apply the credit.

!

Keep a prepaid list

During Tuesday/Friday payment matching, a prepayment looks exactly like a payment with no matching invoice. Keep a short list so you know which are expected and which are genuine orphans.

Moving the date

Customer can move the pickup as many times as he likes, but not more than 14 days past the original start date, and he has to tell you before the original start date. Telling you on the day is not a change — rent has already started.
