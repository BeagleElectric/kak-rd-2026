Stage 1 — the quick look

Two minutes, the day the trailer shows up. Two questions only.

1

Is it dirty?

Washed or not. Any product left in the hoppers. Photo it before anything gets cleaned up.

2

Is there damage?

Walk around it. If there is damage, photo it and give a rough number for what it will cost to fix. A ballpark is fine — Edward just needs something to work with.

3

Customer there? Do it with him

If he happens to be standing there, walk around it together. Most returns are weekends and after hours so usually he is not — that is fine, just do it on your own.

4

Post it to Edward straight away

Unit [UNIT #] is back. Dropped [WHEN]. Dirty: [YES - WHAT / NO] Damage: [WHAT AND WHERE / NONE] Rough cost to fix: $[BALLPARK / N/A] Photos attached.
Photos before cleanup Once it is washed we cannot charge for it. Same with damage — photo it before anything gets touched.

Stage 2 — full inspection

When the shop has time. Same checklist as always.

5

Work the checklist

  • Wash and clean out — all product out of the hoppers
  • Lights, air system, brakes, tires — fill in the measurements
  • Brake lining — replace under 3/8"
  • Tires — replace under 5mm, keep 105–110 psi
  • Rivets, cracks, fenders, landing gear, chutes, tarps
  • Grease everything
  • CVIP — if it expires within 3 months, get a new one on
6

Anything else that turns up

If the full inspection finds damage the quick look missed, post it to Edward the same way. Better late than never.

7

Time and parts on the work order

Keep wash and customer damage itemized separately so Edward can bill it back.

Stage 3 — ready to rent

Clean photos — tick them off

Post them in Signal, not by email The date on the Signal message is the record of when the photos were taken. That is all the timestamp we need.
8

Post that it is ready

Unit [UNIT #] is done and ready to rent. CVIP expires: [DATE] Hubometer: [READING] Work order: [HOURS] hrs, parts $[AMOUNT] Customer damage / wash to bill: [WHAT, or NONE] Photos and checklist attached.
That is it Edward closes off the rental, cancels the payments and bills any damage.
Edward tells you when and who He'll post the unit number, customer name, and pickup date and time in Signal. If you haven't got that, ask him before you hook anything up.

Getting one ready

1

Full rental checklist

Same checklist. Everything filled in, including brake lining and tire measurements.

2

Check the CVIP date

If it expires within 3 months, get a new one on before it goes out.

3

Hook lead and pup together

Test the lights with both hooked up. Split it and either install a nylon slip disk or grease the 5th wheel plate.

4

Paperwork in the document holder

Safety paperwork and registration in the holder on lead and pup. Unit number decals on. Plates on both units.

Photos — about 10, tick them off

5

Post that it's ready

Unit [UNIT #] is ready to go out. CVIP expires: [DATE] Hubometer: [READING] Existing damage on record: [WHAT, or NONE] Photos and checklist attached.

On pickup day

6

Make sure it's accessible

Pulled out and ready for the time Edward gave you.

7

Post when the customer takes it

Unit [UNIT #] picked up by [CUSTOMER] on [DATE] at [TIME].

Anything else
